## Hantavirus clinical reference values

The Global.health line list is the comparison dataset. The separate clinical-reference values are compiled from the published case table, its addendum and WHO risk-assessment material. Field-level values and source links are retained in records/hantavirus/clinical_reference.csv; that CSV does not separately list the addendum URL. The full citations are:

- van den Berg et al., *Andes virus outbreak linked to expedition cruise ship travel, multi-country investigation and response, April to June 2026*. Eurosurveillance 31(24), 2600477 (2026). https://doi.org/10.2807/1560-7917.ES.2026.31.24.2600477
- *Addendum for Euro Surveill. 2026;31(24)*, 9 July 2026. https://doi.org/10.2807/1560-7917.ES.2026.31.27.260709a
- WHO, *Rapid Risk Assessment: Hantavirus outbreak caused by Andes virus, Global v.2*, published 17 May 2026. https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/who-rapid-risk-assessment---hantavirus-outbreak-caused-by-andes-virus--global-v.2

## Other collection and reference reports

Canadian measles reference records retain PHAC report links and explicitly attributed institutional sources. The Sierra Leone mpox files distinguish collection records from WHO reference records and retain the source publications and date evidence. Hantavirus WHO report records retain their release-date counts separately from the clinical reference values. Exact URLs, publishers, dates, source roles and evidence passages are preserved in the case-specific source tables.

The deposited files contain case-study observation records, reference values, source metadata and retained support passages. Original publisher PDF and HTML files are not redistributed. Source publications, quoted passages and third-party datasets retain their original terms; their inclusion does not imply a new package-wide licence. No package-wide reuse licence is specified for the authors' extracted records.
